# Design Documents

> Design decisions and specifications.

## What belongs in a design document

- The problem being solved and who it affects.
- The proposed approach and the alternatives that were rejected, with reasons.
- Constraints and assumptions.
- Consequences, including operational cost and maintenance burden.
- A decision log, so the reasoning survives the people who were in the room.

## Why they matter

Most disputes about a Power BI solution come from an undocumented assumption
rather than a technical disagreement. Recording the decision and its rationale
prevents the same trade-off being re-litigated later.
